# Northwind Retail Co. — Workplace Conduct Policy

**Doc ID:** workplace-conduct-policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture Team and Legal
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and interns.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets expectations for professional conduct, defines prohibited behavior, and describes how concerns are reported and investigated.

## 2. Core Expectations

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and vendors with respect and professionalism; comply with all applicable laws and Company policies; perform their duties honestly; and avoid conflicts of interest that are not disclosed and approved in advance.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

Prohibited conduct includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment or discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, veteran status, or any other legally protected characteristic.
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation.
- Workplace violence, threats, or intimidation.
- Theft, fraud, or falsification of Company records (including expense reports and timekeeping).
- Being under the influence of alcohol or illegal drugs while on duty (except at Company-sanctioned events with responsible consumption).
- Unauthorized disclosure of Confidential information, as defined in the Data Security Policy (`data-security-policy`).
- Misuse of Company equipment, systems, or corporate cards for purposes outside policy (see Equipment Policy `equipment-policy` and Expense Policy `expense-policy`).

## 4. Reporting a Concern

Employees who experience or witness a violation of this policy should report it through one or more of the following channels: their direct manager, any People & Culture Business Partner, the confidential ethics hotline, or (for security-specific incidents) the IT Security incident channel described in the Data Security Policy. Reports may be made anonymously where permitted by local law, though anonymity may limit the ability to investigate fully.

## 5. Investigation Process

- People & Culture, in coordination with Legal when appropriate, will investigate reported concerns promptly, generally beginning within 5 business days of receipt.
- Investigations are handled with as much confidentiality as is practical, on a need-to-know basis.
- Both the reporting party and the subject of a complaint will be treated fairly, and the process is not intended to presume guilt before investigation is complete.
- Interim measures (e.g., temporary schedule changes, no-contact directives) may be put in place during an investigation to protect the parties involved.

## 6. Consequences

Substantiated violations may result in a range of consequences depending on severity, from a documented coaching conversation, to a formal written warning, to suspension, to termination of employment. Serious violations (e.g., violence, harassment, theft, willful data security breaches) may result in immediate termination and, where applicable, referral to law enforcement.

## 7. Non-Retaliation

Northwind strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee for reporting a good-faith concern or participating in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a violation of this policy and subject to the same consequences described in Section 6.
