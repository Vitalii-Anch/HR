# Northwind Retail Co. — Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Doc ID:** expense-policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** Finance Team
**Applies To:** All employees who incur business expenses on behalf of Northwind.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains what business expenses are reimbursable, how to submit them, and the approval thresholds that apply.

## 2. General Principles

Expenses must be **reasonable, necessary, and directly related** to Company business. Employees should act as though spending their own money: choose economical options and avoid excess. Northwind does not reimburse expenses that are primarily personal in nature, even if incurred during a business trip.

## 3. Categories of Reimbursable Expenses

- **Travel:** Coach-class airfare, standard rail fare, mileage (at the current IRS standard mileage rate for U.S. employees), parking, tolls, and reasonable rideshare/taxi fares for business travel.
- **Lodging:** Hotel or comparable lodging at a reasonable rate for the destination city; employees should use the Company's preferred travel booking tool when available.
- **Meals:** Reasonable meal costs while traveling on Company business, up to a daily cap of $75 USD (or local equivalent) per employee, itemized where possible. Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a client entertainment meal and pre-approved by a manager.
- **Client Entertainment:** Reasonable costs entertaining clients or candidates, with a stated business purpose and list of attendees, requires manager pre-approval above $200.
- **Home Office Stipend:** Remote and hybrid employees (see Remote Work Policy, `remote-work-policy`) receive an annual home-office stipend of $500 USD for items such as a desk, chair, or monitor, submitted as a reimbursement with receipts.
- **Professional Development:** Conference fees, certifications, and job-related courses up to $1,500 USD per year with manager approval.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, personal grooming, airline lounge memberships (unless separately approved for a specific role), spousal/partner travel costs, and any expense lacking an itemized receipt over $25 are not reimbursable.

## 5. Submission and Approval

1. Submit expense reports within **30 days** of the expense being incurred, via the Company expense system, with itemized receipts attached for any single expense over $25.
2. Expense reports are routed to the employee's direct manager for approval.
3. Expenses **under $500** require single manager approval.
4. Expenses **$500–$2,500** require manager approval plus Department Director approval.
5. Expenses **over $2,500** require manager, Director, and Finance Business Partner approval before submission, not after.
6. Reimbursement is issued via payroll or direct deposit within 2 pay cycles of final approval.

## 6. Corporate Card Usage

Employees issued a Northwind corporate card must use it for business travel and client entertainment where possible, and must submit supporting documentation for every transaction within the same 30-day window as personal reimbursements. Misuse of a corporate card for personal expenses may result in card revocation and disciplinary action per the Workplace Conduct Policy (`workplace-conduct-policy`).

## 7. International Travel

International travel over $1,000 in projected cost requires pre-trip approval from the employee's Director. Currency conversion for reimbursement uses the exchange rate on the date the expense was incurred, as reported by the Company's finance system.
