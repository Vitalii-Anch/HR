NORTHWIND RETAIL CO. -- COMPENSATION AND PAY POLICY

Doc ID: compensation-pay-policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: Finance and People & Culture (Total Rewards)
Applies To: All employees.

SECTION 1: PURPOSE

This policy explains how pay is set, how pay periods and pay dates work, and how compensation changes (merit, promotion, market adjustment) are administered.

SECTION 2: PAY STRUCTURE

Northwind uses defined pay bands for each job level, benchmarked annually against retail and corporate industry survey data. Base pay is set within the applicable band based on experience, skills, and internal equity. Retail hourly roles (Store Associate, Warehouse Operator) are paid hourly and are eligible for overtime under applicable law. Corporate salaried roles at Level 4 and above are generally classified as exempt from overtime, consistent with local wage and hour law; classification is determined by HR and Legal, not by the employee or manager individually.

SECTION 3: PAY PERIODS AND PAYDAYS

Northwind pays employees on a biweekly schedule (26 pay periods per year). Paychecks are issued via direct deposit on every other Friday. Hourly employees must submit timesheets by end of day on the Monday following the close of the pay period; late timesheet submission may delay payment to the following pay cycle.

SECTION 4: OVERTIME

Non-exempt (hourly) employees are paid overtime at 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek, or as required by state law if state law is more generous (for example, daily overtime thresholds in some states). Overtime must be pre-approved by a manager except in genuine operational emergencies, but even unapproved overtime hours actually worked must still be paid in full; pre-approval affects manager accountability, not employee pay.

SECTION 5: MERIT INCREASES, PROMOTIONS, AND MARKET ADJUSTMENTS

Merit increases are generally awarded once per year following the Annual Review cycle described in the Performance Management Policy (doc id: performance-management-policy), effective each April. Promotions may occur at any time during the year when a role change is approved, with an associated pay adjustment to place the employee appropriately within the new role's pay band. Market adjustments (pay changes not tied to a promotion or merit cycle, made to correct a pay band misalignment) require Director and Total Rewards approval.

SECTION 6: BONUS AND INCENTIVE PROGRAMS

Eligible corporate employees may participate in an annual bonus program with a target payout expressed as a percentage of base salary, determined by role level and Company performance against annual financial goals. Retail hourly employees may participate in store-level incentive programs tied to sales and customer satisfaction metrics, paid quarterly. Bonus and incentive payouts are not guaranteed and are subject to Company and individual performance.

SECTION 7: PAY EQUITY AND TRANSPARENCY

Northwind conducts an annual pay equity audit reviewing pay by gender, race, and other protected characteristics within comparable roles and locations, and takes corrective action where unexplained disparities are identified. Employees may discuss their own pay openly; retaliation against employees for discussing their own compensation is prohibited under the Workplace Conduct Policy (doc id: workplace-conduct-policy).
