Northwind Retail Co. — Performance Management Policy

Doc ID: performance-management-policy
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Owner: People & Culture Team
Applies To: All full-time and part-time employees.

1. Purpose

This policy describes how performance is set, reviewed, and documented at Northwind, including the annual review cycle, goal-setting, and the performance improvement process.

2. Goal Setting

Employees set annual goals with their manager within the first 30 days of each calendar year (or within 30 days of hire for new employees), aligned to team and department objectives. Goals are documented in the HR system and revisited at least at the mid-year checkpoint described in Section 3.

3. Review Cycle

  • Mid-Year Check-In (July): An informal conversation between manager and employee to review progress against goals; no formal rating is assigned.
  • Annual Review (January): A formal written review covering the prior calendar year, including a performance rating on a 5-point scale (Exceeds, Strong, Meets, Partially Meets, Does Not Meet), which factors into merit increase and bonus decisions.
  • New Hire 90-Day Review: As described in the Onboarding Checklist (onboarding-checklist), a check-in at 90 days to confirm the new hire is meeting initial expectations.

4. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

When an employee receives a rating of "Partially Meets" or "Does Not Meet," or when a manager identifies a significant, sustained performance gap outside the annual cycle, the manager, in consultation with People & Culture, may initiate a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

A PIP typically runs for 30 to 60 days and includes: specific, measurable expectations; a defined timeline; and scheduled check-ins (at least biweekly). At the end of the PIP period, the manager documents whether expectations were met, partially met, or not met, which may lead to continued employment, an extension, or separation, consistent with the Workplace Conduct Policy (workplace-conduct-policy) where conduct issues are also present.

5. Promotions and Merit Increases

Promotion and merit increase decisions are informed by the Annual Review rating, budget availability, and calibration across the department to ensure consistency. Managers submit promotion recommendations to their Director for approval; merit increases are typically effective each April following the January review cycle.

6. Documentation and Records

All formal review documents, ratings, and PIP records are retained in the employee's HR file for the duration of employment plus the period required by applicable record-retention law, and are treated as Confidential per the Data Security Policy (data-security-policy).
