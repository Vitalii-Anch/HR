# Northwind Retail Co. — Equipment Policy

**Doc ID:** equipment-policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** IT Department
**Applies To:** All employees issued Company equipment; contractors per engagement terms.

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes what equipment Northwind provides, how it is requested, and employee responsibilities for care and return.

## 2. Standard Equipment by Role Category

- **Corporate (Fully Remote / Hybrid) roles:** Company laptop, headset, and a one-time home office stipend (see Expense Policy, `expense-policy`, Section 3) for a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and furniture. Hybrid employees may also be issued a docking station for office use.
- **Retail (On-Site) roles:** Access to shared store hardware (POS terminals, handheld scanners); personal equipment issuance is limited to a Company-branded uniform allowance, not computing equipment, unless the role includes back-office administrative duties.
- **Warehouse/Distribution roles:** Access to shared warehouse management terminals and handheld scanners; no individually issued laptop unless the role is supervisory.

## 3. Requesting Equipment

New hire equipment is provisioned automatically by IT as part of onboarding (see `onboarding-checklist`). Additional or replacement equipment requests are submitted through the IT service desk and require manager approval for any item over $300 in value.

## 4. Care, Loss, and Damage

- Employees are responsible for reasonable care of Company equipment.
- Loss or theft must be reported to IT within 24 hours (consistent with the Data Security Policy, `data-security-policy`, Section 5), and a police report may be required for insurance purposes.
- Accidental damage during normal use is covered by IT's standard replacement process at no cost to the employee. Repeated loss or damage due to negligence may result in the cost being charged back to the employee's department, at manager discretion.

## 5. Personal Use

Reasonable personal use of Company-issued laptops (e.g., checking personal email) is permitted but must not violate the Data Security Policy or Workplace Conduct Policy, and personal use should not interfere with business use or introduce security risk (e.g., installing unapproved software).

## 6. Return of Equipment

All Company equipment must be returned within 5 business days of an employee's last day of employment, using the prepaid shipping label provided by IT for remote employees, or in person for on-site employees. Unreturned equipment above $100 in value may be deducted from final pay where permitted by state law, or invoiced separately.

## 7. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Limited BYOD is permitted for checking email and calendar via approved mobile device management (MDM) enrollment, but Confidential data (per the Data Security Policy) may not be stored on personal devices, and MDM enrollment is mandatory to enable this access.
