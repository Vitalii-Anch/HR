# Northwind Retail Co. — Leave Policy (Parental and Medical)

**Doc ID:** leave-policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture Team, in coordination with Legal
**Applies To:** Full-time and eligible part-time employees. This policy summarizes Company-provided leave; it works alongside, and does not reduce, any leave required by federal, state, or local law (e.g., FMLA in the U.S.).

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes Northwind's parental leave and medical (disability) leave programs, which are distinct from PTO (see `pto-policy`) and are intended for longer, protected absences.

## 2. Eligibility

- Employees become eligible for Company-paid parental and medical leave after **90 days of continuous full-time or part-time (20+ hrs/week) employment**.
- Employees with less than 90 days of tenure may still be eligible for unpaid, job-protected leave under applicable law (e.g., state short-term disability programs), administered by Payroll/Benefits on a case-by-case basis.
- Contractors are not eligible for Company-paid leave under this policy.

## 3. Parental Leave

- **Birthing parents** receive **12 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of base pay, which may run concurrently with any state-mandated disability leave for childbirth recovery.
- **Non-birthing parents** (including adoptive and foster parents) receive **6 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of base pay.
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth, adoption, or foster placement and may be taken continuously or in up to two blocks with manager and HR agreement.
- Employees should notify their manager and HR at least **30 days before** the expected leave start date when the need is foreseeable (e.g., a due date), or as soon as practicable when it is not.

## 4. Medical (Disability) Leave

- Employees unable to work due to their own serious health condition are eligible for **Company-paid medical leave for up to 10 weeks**, coordinated with any applicable state short-term disability benefit (Northwind pays the difference up to 100% of base pay where state benefits are lower, up to the 10-week cap).
- Medical leave beyond 10 weeks may continue as unpaid, job-protected leave where required by law, or by manager/HR approval for exceptional circumstances.
- A healthcare provider's certification is required for medical leaves longer than 5 consecutive business days.

## 5. Interaction with PTO and Benefits

- Parental and medical leave run separately from PTO; employees are not required to exhaust PTO before or during a qualifying leave, though they may choose to use PTO for partial-pay gaps (e.g., a non-birthing parent's leave beyond 6 weeks) subject to manager approval.
- Health benefits continue uninterrupted during Company-paid leave, with the employee continuing to pay their normal share of premiums via payroll deduction or direct billing.
- PTO continues to accrue during the first 12 weeks of any Company-paid leave; accrual pauses for unpaid leave extensions beyond that point.

## 6. Return to Work

Employees returning from parental or medical leave are restored to their same or an equivalent role, consistent with applicable law. Employees anticipating a phased or part-time return should discuss options with their manager and HR at least 2 weeks before the planned return date.

## 7. Other Protected Leave

Northwind also provides leave for jury duty, bereavement (up to 5 paid days for immediate family, 3 for extended family), and military service consistent with applicable law. Contact HR for specifics not covered in this summary.
