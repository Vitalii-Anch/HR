# Northwind Retail Co. — Benefits Overview

**Doc ID:** benefits-overview
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture — Total Rewards Team
**Applies To:** Full-time and part-time employees; contractors are not eligible for Company benefits.

## 1. Purpose

This document summarizes health, retirement, and supplemental benefits available to Northwind employees. It is a summary; full plan documents are held by the Total Rewards team and govern in case of conflict.

## 2. Eligibility

- **Full-time employees** are eligible for the full benefits suite starting the **first of the month following 30 days of employment**.
- **Part-time employees** working 20+ hours/week are eligible for a limited benefits package (medical only, no dental/vision) starting after 60 days of employment.
- **Part-time employees under 20 hours/week and contractors** are not eligible for Company-sponsored benefits.

## 3. Health Plans

Northwind offers three medical plan tiers administered by a third-party carrier:

- **Core PPO:** Lower premium, higher deductible ($2,000 individual / $4,000 family).
- **Plus PPO:** Higher premium, lower deductible ($750 individual / $1,500 family).
- **HDHP + HSA:** High-deductible health plan paired with a Health Savings Account; Northwind contributes $500/year (individual) or $1,000/year (family) to the HSA.

Dental and vision coverage are available as add-ons for full-time employees at modest additional cost, bundled at open enrollment.

## 4. Retirement — 401(k)

Full-time and part-time (20+ hrs/week) employees are eligible to contribute to the Company 401(k) plan starting on day one of employment. Northwind matches **100% of the first 3%** of eligible compensation contributed, and **50% of the next 2%** (i.e., a maximum match of 4% of pay for a 5% employee contribution). Matching contributions vest immediately.

## 5. Life and Disability Insurance

Full-time employees receive Company-paid basic life insurance (1x annual salary) and short-term disability coverage at no cost. Voluntary supplemental life insurance (up to 5x salary) is available at employee expense.

## 6. Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

All employees (including part-time and, for crisis counseling only, contractors) have access to a confidential Employee Assistance Program offering counseling sessions, financial planning consultations, and legal referrals at no cost.

## 7. Open Enrollment and Life Events

Open enrollment occurs annually in November for coverage effective January 1. Outside of open enrollment, employees may change benefits elections only after a **qualifying life event** (marriage, divorce, birth/adoption of a child, loss of other coverage) within 30 days of the event.
