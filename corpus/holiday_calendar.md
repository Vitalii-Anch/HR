# Northwind Retail Co. — Holiday Calendar Policy

**Doc ID:** holiday-calendar
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies To:** All employees (full-time, part-time); contractors follow their engagement agreement.

## 1. Purpose

This policy lists Company-observed paid holidays and explains how holiday pay is calculated for different employment types and how holidays interact with PTO and retail scheduling.

## 2. Observed Holidays (United States)

Northwind observes the following paid holidays for U.S.-based employees in 2026:

| Holiday | Date |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | January 1 |
| Memorial Day | May 25 |
| Juneteenth | June 19 |
| Independence Day | July 3 (observed) |
| Labor Day | September 7 |
| Thanksgiving Day | November 26 |
| Day After Thanksgiving | November 27 |
| Winter Holiday (Christmas Eve/Day) | December 24–25 |

Employees outside the United States follow a locally published holiday calendar maintained by their regional People & Culture partner, reflecting local statutory holidays. Contact your regional HR partner for the country-specific list.

## 3. Retail Store Staffing on Holidays

Because Northwind operates retail storefronts, certain roles (Store Associate, Store Manager, Assistant Store Manager) may be scheduled to work on a subset of observed holidays due to business needs, particularly Thanksgiving weekend and the winter holiday season. Employees required to work an observed holiday receive **holiday premium pay at 1.5x their regular hourly rate** for hours worked on that day, plus the ability to take a substitute floating holiday within 60 days.

Corporate, warehouse, and remote employees are generally not scheduled on observed holidays.

## 4. Floating Holidays

Full-time employees receive **2 floating holidays per calendar year**, which may be used for religious observances, cultural celebrations, or personal preference not covered by the standard calendar. Floating holidays do not carry over between years and are not paid out on separation. Floating holidays are requested the same way as PTO (see PTO Policy, `pto-policy`) but do not draw down the PTO balance.

## 5. Holiday Pay Calculation

- Full-time employees receive their standard daily pay for each observed holiday they are not scheduled to work.
- Part-time employees receive holiday pay pro-rated based on their average scheduled hours over the preceding 90 days.
- Contractors do not receive holiday pay under this policy.

## 6. Holidays Falling on Weekends

When an observed holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday, unless local retail scheduling needs require an alternate observed date, which will be communicated at least 30 days in advance by the Store Operations team.
