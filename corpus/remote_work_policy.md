# Northwind Retail Co. — Remote Work Policy

**Doc ID:** remote-work-policy
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture Team, in coordination with IT Security and Payroll/Tax
**Applies To:** Corporate (non-retail-floor) employees whose roles are designated as remote-eligible.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines who is eligible to work remotely, what approvals are required, and what tax, security, and equipment obligations apply. It works together with the Data Security Policy (`data-security-policy`) and Equipment Policy (`equipment-policy`).

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined by role, not by employee preference alone. Eligible role categories:

- **Fully Remote:** Roles explicitly designated fully remote in the job requisition (e.g., many Software Engineering, Data, and certain Corporate Support roles). No physical office attendance is required.
- **Hybrid:** Roles that require in-office presence a minimum of 2 days per week at an assigned Northwind office (e.g., most Marketing, Finance, and People & Culture corporate roles).
- **On-Site Required:** Roles requiring physical presence at a retail store, distribution center, or specific facility (e.g., Store Associate, Warehouse Operator, Store Manager). These roles are **not** remote-eligible under this policy.

Employees should confirm their role's remote designation with their manager or in the HR system; designation is set at the role level during headcount planning, not negotiated individually except through the exception process in Section 5.

## 3. Manager and Location Approval

- Employees in Fully Remote or Hybrid roles who wish to work from a location other than their originally assigned home office state/country must obtain **written manager approval** before relocating, because of payroll tax and legal-entity implications (see Section 4).
- Requests to work remotely from a **different U.S. state** than the one on file require manager approval and a Payroll tax-registration check, since Northwind must withhold and remit payroll taxes correctly in the employee's actual work state.
- Requests to work **outside the United States** require additional approval from People & Culture leadership and Legal, because Northwind may not have a registered legal entity or tax presence in every country, and immigration/labor law compliance must be reviewed case by case. Cross-border remote work is not automatically approved and, in many cases, is denied unless Northwind has established a compliant local employment mechanism.
- Temporary remote work (e.g., visiting family for up to 10 consecutive business days within the same country) does not require the formal location-change approval but should be communicated to the employee's manager in advance.

## 4. Tax and Legal Considerations

Working from a different tax jurisdiction than the one on file can create employer withholding obligations, permanent-establishment risk for the Company, and unemployment insurance registration requirements. For this reason:

- Employees must keep their official "work location" in the HR system accurate and up to date.
- Any change in work-from state or country must be reported to HR **before** the move, not after.
- Failure to disclose a location change may result in incorrect tax withholding, which is the employee's responsibility to reconcile, and may also constitute a policy violation subject to corrective action.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions to role-based remote eligibility (e.g., a Hybrid-designated employee requesting Fully Remote status) require a written business justification, manager approval, and sign-off from the relevant Department VP. Exceptions are reviewed quarterly and are not guaranteed to be renewed.

## 6. Security and Equipment Requirements for Remote Work

All remote and hybrid employees must comply with the Data Security Policy (`data-security-policy`), including use of Company-issued or Company-approved devices, VPN for accessing internal systems, and encrypted storage for any documents containing employee or customer personal data. See the Equipment Policy (`equipment-policy`) for hardware provisioning rules for home offices.
