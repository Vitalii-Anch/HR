# Northwind Retail Co. — New Hire Onboarding Checklist

**Doc ID:** onboarding-checklist
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Owner:** People & Culture Team
**Applies To:** All new full-time and part-time hires. Contractor onboarding follows an abbreviated version (Section 6).

## 1. Before Day One

- HR sends the offer letter, background check consent, and I-9/employment eligibility forms.
- IT provisions accounts (email, HR system, Slack-equivalent) and ships equipment per the Equipment Policy (`equipment-policy`) for remote/hybrid hires, or prepares an on-site workstation.
- Manager assigns an onboarding buddy and schedules a Day One welcome meeting.
- Payroll sets up direct deposit and tax withholding forms (W-4 or local equivalent) for the new hire to complete on or before Day One.

## 2. Day One

- New hire attends a Company orientation session covering mission, values, and key policies (this policy corpus mirrors the topics covered).
- New hire completes remaining I-9 verification, benefits enrollment instructions, and reviews the Workplace Conduct Policy (`workplace-conduct-policy`) and Data Security Policy (`data-security-policy`), acknowledging receipt electronically.
- IT confirms device setup, VPN access (if remote/hybrid), and MFA enrollment.
- Manager reviews 30/60/90-day expectations and the employee's initial goals.

## 3. First Week

- Complete role-specific systems training (POS systems for retail roles; internal tools for corporate roles).
- Enroll in benefits within the eligibility window described in the Benefits Overview (`benefits-overview`) — note that coverage begins the first of the month following 30 days of employment for full-time hires.
- Meet with HR Business Partner to review PTO accrual (see `pto-policy`) and confirm work location for tax purposes (see `remote-work-policy`).
- For remote/hybrid hires, confirm home office security setup per the Data Security Policy (`data-security-policy`).

## 4. First 30 Days

- Complete all required compliance training (harassment prevention, data security awareness, code of conduct).
- Manager conducts a 30-day check-in to review early performance and answer policy questions.
- Confirm 401(k) enrollment decision (auto-enrollment defaults apply if no election is made within 30 days — see Benefits Overview).

## 5. First 90 Days

- Manager conducts a 90-day review, formally ending the initial ramp-up period.
- HR confirms benefits elections are finalized and PTO balance reflects correct accrual to date.
- New hire onboarding is marked complete in the HR system.

## 6. Contractor Onboarding (Abbreviated)

Contractors complete an abbreviated onboarding: signed independent contractor agreement, IT account provisioning limited to project-necessary systems, a Data Security Policy briefing, and confirmation of engagement scope and end date with their sponsoring manager. Contractors do not complete benefits enrollment or receive a PTO briefing, as they are not eligible for either (see `benefits-overview`, `pto-policy`).
